# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Forma UI a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, nationality, disability or technical preference.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and specific.
- Assume good intent while taking impact seriously.
- Accept technical disagreement without personal attacks.
- Give actionable feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing personal or confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized attention, sustained personal attacks, or deliberate disruption of project spaces are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove violating content, close discussions, or restrict participation. Reports should be sent privately through the repository's configured contact channels. Maintainers should explain enforcement decisions when doing so does not compromise reporter privacy or safety.
